Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how YonoApp.in (“we”, “our”) handles information when you visit https://yonoapp.in. We operate an independent directory of third-party app listings. We do not require an account to browse.

1. Who we are

YonoApp.in is an informational website. We are not the owner or operator of the gaming applications linked from our pages. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

2. Information we may receive

Like most websites, when you visit us our hosting provider (for example GitHub Pages) or security tools may automatically process technical data such as:

  • IP address and approximate region
  • Browser type, device type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, date and time of visit
  • Referring URL (if any)

We do not ask you to create a login on YonoApp.in. We do not intentionally collect bank details, Aadhaar, PAN, or game-wallet passwords on this site.

3. How we use information

  • To keep the website online, secure and working
  • To understand basic traffic patterns and fix errors
  • To respond if you email us

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Cookies and similar technology

Essential technology may be used for basic site function. If analytics or similar tools are enabled in future, they may store their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-party apps and links

When you tap Download or open a referral path, you leave YonoApp.in and interact with a third-party operator. That operator may collect mobile numbers, KYC documents, payment data and gameplay records under their policy. YonoApp.in does not control that data.

Telegram and WhatsApp channels linked from this site are messaging platforms with their own privacy terms. Use them only if you accept those platforms’ rules.

6. Data retention

Server and security logs are typically kept only as long as needed for operations and abuse prevention, subject to the hosting provider’s practices.

7. Children’s privacy

This website is for adults 18+ only. We do not knowingly target children. If you believe a minor has used the site, contact us and we will take reasonable steps.

8. Your choices

  • You can stop using the site at any time
